Night light · Bar and panel

DuskNew

A night light that follows the sun and knows when to step aside.

Warm evenings from your real sunset, daylight back at sunrise, and a fade too slow to notice. One click in the bar for everything else.

GitHub

What only Dusk does

Why this one

  • It follows the sun where you are.Sunrise and sunset are worked out on the laptop from your location, with no account and no network, and they move with the seasons and the clock change on their own.
  • You never see the switch.The colour changes in steps too small for the eye to catch, over forty minutes by default, so the room just seems to get cosier. No sudden orange flash at sunset.
  • It gets out of the way.Name the apps that need true colour, like a game or a photo editor, or every fullscreen app, and the screen goes neutral while one is in front, then warms again when you leave it.
  • It respects your hand on the switch.Press Omarchy's own night light key and Dusk takes that as your choice until the next sunset or sunrise, instead of fighting you. A dot on its icon shows when something other than the schedule is in charge.
  • Darker than the panel goes.For the small hours, dim below the screen's lowest brightness, along with the warmth.

Built on hyprsunset, which Omarchy already ships. Works offline, needs no root, and writes nothing but its own settings.

Using it

How you reach it

Right click itOff until morning, or back to the schedule
Warm for 1 hEvening colour now, then back to the schedule
Add the app in frontNeutral colour whenever that app is focused
