Face unlock for Omarchy

PenguID

Look at your laptop and you're in. The infrared camera recognizes you on the lock screen, at sudo and in polkit prompts, and a small penguin above every window shows what it is doing, the way Face ID does on an iPhone.

  • Face data stays on the laptop, encrypted under a key sealed by the TPM.
  • Face ID-style password rules: after boot, every 48 hours, and after 5 refused faces.
  • Nothing scans until you press Enter at a prompt.
