KDE Plasma 6

On Plasma tooIn testing

Nine of the Omarchy plugins, as Plasma widgets.

The same work underneath: the same helpers, recorders and samplers, with a panel or tray widget in Plasma's own controls and colours on top. Where Plasma already does something well, they work with it instead of beside it: Dusk drives Plasma's Night Light, Reserve sits next to its battery widget. They are being tested on a real Plasma desktop now, and install from each repository's plasma branch until they are on the KDE Store.

Typing into other apps

How the emoji picker and Mynah type on Plasma

Plasma does not let ordinary programs pretend to be a keyboard, which is how typing works on Omarchy. So on Plasma they do what a person would: the text goes onto the clipboard, paste is pressed through Plasma's own remote-input permission (Ctrl+Shift+V in a terminal, Ctrl+V elsewhere), and your clipboard is put back a moment later. Plasma asks once whether that is allowed; tick “Allow without asking next time” and it never asks again. Anything that passes through the clipboard appears in Plasma's clipboard history, so dictated text does too.

Not on Plasma

What stays on Omarchy, and why

  • Clipboard shelfPlasma's own clipboard already pins items: star one in its history and it stays.
  • Idle dimPlasma's Power Management already dims the screen before it turns off.
  • Barber, Tile blueprints, Aerial, Tactile, OmalangThey reshape Hyprland and Omarchy's bar themselves; on Plasma, System Settings and KWin do those jobs.
  • Omagram and PenguIDLater: Omagram's window would have to be rewritten for Plasma, and face unlock needs Plasma's lock screen and PAM work first.
